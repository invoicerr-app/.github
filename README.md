## What Invoicerr is

Open-source invoicing you can host yourself or use in our cloud: quotes, invoices, payments and the
paperwork that follows, including the e-invoicing rules of the country you bill from.

- One document engine for quotes, invoices, credit notes, purchase orders, expenses and received
  invoices.
- Payments, bank reconciliation, a client portal, time tracking and recurring documents.
- E-invoice XML and national channels (PDP, Chorus Pro, KSeF, SdI), with Germany, France, Italy, Poland
  and Portugal covered as data, not code.
- Free to self-host with Docker or Helm. The cloud version starts at $15 a month after a 14-day trial.

The full feature list, screenshots and the quick start are in the
[application's README](https://github.com/invoicerr-app/invoicerr#readme).
